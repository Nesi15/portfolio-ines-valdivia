About me

I combine an engineering background with an audiovisual communication eye to streamline workflows: I build custom tools to organise, analyse, plan and automate tasks at every stage of the process.

  • From brief to shooting plan

    Full pre-production of the Guna Yala documentary, the "Gente como tú" campaign for Cáritas and the short film about the Cabanyal-Cañamelar neighbourhood: requirements, schedule, locations and crew.

  • Coordinating crews and suppliers

    Coordination with artists, technical crew and suppliers at the Clazz festival, and production of multi-screen content for Prosegur's immersive room.

  • Post-production

    Editing in DaVinci Resolve, Avid Media Composer and Premiere, animation in After Effects and AI image and video generation in ComfyUI.

  • Producing on tight resources

    Co-founder of the university production company Raval Producciones, content production for the Inspiring Girls Foundation and against-the-clock shoots such as "Raspado", shot and edited in a single day.
